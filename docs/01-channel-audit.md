## 2. First impression and positioning

**This channel is not blank. It already has a sharp, defensible concept, and the name is the concept.**
In engineering, a *rating* is the load, speed, pressure or temperature something is certified for.
"Past the rating" is exactly what happens in a failure, a test to destruction, or a survival story.
Very few channel names are this literal about their premise.

What's strong:
- **Name ↔ premise fit is excellent.** A viewer can predict what the channel does from the name alone.
- **The logo already says it:** a square (a limit or box) broken by an orange slash (a breach). It's simple and works at 48 px.
- The description has a usable signature line: *"How far past the rating can it really go?"*
- "Built around the numbers" is a real differentiator against the wave of vague faceless "disaster" channels (§ competitor research).

What's weak or missing:
- 0 uploads, so the algorithm has no audience model for the channel.
- Country is unset, which matters for YPP eligibility and for ad targeting.
- No trailer, no playlists, no links, no featured sections.
- The description promises "short-form" only. That is the wrong commitment, because Shorts views never count toward watch hours (see monetization doc).
- "New episodes every week" undersells the plan we'll actually run.

## 4. Copyright and reused-content risk

- No uploads yet, so the slate is clean.
- **Main risk ahead:** the niche is full of channels that put others' news footage on screen under a "fair use notice" (e.g. Global Weather Network's description). YouTube's 2026 *inauthentic content* enforcement targets mass-produced, low-human-input channels. **Rule for us: no third-party footage as the main visual.** We use our own motion graphics and diagrams, US-government public-domain investigation material (e.g. NBS/NIST reports), properly licensed stock, and clearly labelled AI reconstructions only where needed.
- Realistic AI depictions of real events must be disclosed using the **"Altered or synthetic content"** checkbox at upload.
