# Deliverable 6 — 7-Day Content & Publishing Calendar (Week 1: 16–22 Nov 2026)

## Publishing ratio, and why
**Week 1: 6 Shorts + 1 long-form. Steady state from week 2: 5 Shorts + 2 long-form per week.**
- Shorts views **never** count toward the 4,000/3,000 watch-hour requirement. Shorts are our *subscriber acquisition* and *audience-learning* engine (cheap tests of which topics hit).
- Long-form is the *only* route to watch hours and later the main ad revenue (mid-rolls from 8 min).
- One excellent long-form in week 1 beats two rushed ones. Week 2 adds a second long-form once the pipeline is proven.
- Every Short links to a long-form via the Shorts **"Related video"** field as soon as one exists.

## Publish times (IST) and rationale
- **Shorts: 7:30 PM IST** (= 9:00 AM US Eastern, 2:00 PM UK). This catches the Western morning and the Indian evening in one slot.
- **Long-form: Saturday 8:30 PM IST** (= 10:00 AM US Eastern Saturday, 3:00 PM UK).
- These are starting hypotheses. After 2–3 weeks, replace them with Studio's *"When your viewers are on YouTube"* data.

## SH-04 — Silver Bridge (Thu 19 Nov)
**Title:** `A Crack This Small Dropped an Entire Bridge` · **Cover:** `2.5 MM` (orange)
**Script (≈ 92 words):**
> A crack about as deep as a coin is thick brought down an entire bridge.
> December 1967. Rush hour on the Silver Bridge, between Ohio and West Virginia.
> It didn't hang from cables. It hung from chains of steel eyebars, linked like a bicycle chain, just two bars per link.
> Inside one joint, a tiny crack had been growing for years, hidden where no inspector could see it.
> That evening, it split. With no backup path for the load, the bridge folded into the river.
> Forty-six people died.
> America's national bridge inspection standards exist because of this collapse.

**Visuals:** eyebar-chain diagram, zoom into the pin joint, crack in orange; Rating Bar labelled `REDUNDANCY: 0`. **Fact-check:** 15 Dec 1967; Point Pleasant WV ↔ Kanauga OH; eyebar 330; crack ≈ 0.1 in (≈ 2.5 mm) from stress corrosion; 46 deaths; NBIS 1971 (NTSB report HAR-71-01).
**SEO:** silver bridge collapse, eyebar failure, bridge collapse explained, fracture critical bridge, point pleasant.

## SH-05 — IP68 (Fri 20 Nov)
**Title:** `IP68 Doesn't Mean What You Think` · **Cover:** `IP68` with `6 / 8` decoded
**Script (≈ 95 words):**
> Your phone is rated IP68. Your warranty still won't cover water damage.
> Here's what the rating actually says. The six: dust-tight. The eight: survives immersion beyond one metre, under conditions the manufacturer chooses.
> Those tests happen in a lab. Fresh, still water. A brand-new phone. Room temperature.
> Your pool is chlorinated. The sea is salty. A shower is hot, moving water and steam. And the seals age every time you drop it.
> That's why manufacturers say water resistance isn't permanent.
> The rating is real. It's just rated for a lab, not your life.

**Visuals:** "IP68" splits into 6 and 8 with labels; lab tank vs pool/sea/shower icons; seal ageing animation. **Fact-check:** IEC 60529 definitions; Apple's and Samsung's support pages on water resistance and warranty. **SEO:** ip68 meaning, is ip68 waterproof, ip68 vs ip67, phone water damage warranty. **Monetization note:** high-RPM consumer-tech topic; later affiliate links to waterproof pouches/cases.
