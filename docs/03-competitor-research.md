## C. Patterns behind the winners → our rules

1. **Hook = a concrete object plus a violated expectation** ("destroyed on purpose", "made things worse", "survived 75 floors"). Never a question about the viewer.
2. **One mechanism per Short.** Each winning Short explains exactly one cause.
3. **Dates and places raise trust and CTR** ("Glen Canyon Dam, 1983").
4. **Short Shorts win completion.** The 31-second Leaning Tower Short had the fastest view velocity. Our 30–40 s rule is supported by the data.
5. **Diagrams beat stock footage** in engineering explainers (ConstrucBIM, Plainly Difficult). Our motion-graphic "Rating Bar" format fits this.
6. **Respectful framing scales.** The breakout disaster channels explicitly promise "no gore, no fearmongering".
7. **Gap we exploit: nobody leads with the number.** Competitors tell the story and mention numbers in passing. We put the rated-vs-actual number on screen in the first second, every time. That is our recognisable format.
