# Video 001 — Research & Fact-Check Record

**Subject:** Hyatt Regency Kansas City skywalk collapse, 17 July 1981
**Checked:** 15 Nov 2026 · **Method:** web search against primary/official sources where available. The sandbox network couldn't open NIST's PDF directly, so NBS findings were verified through the NIST publication record, search-indexed text of the report, and independent secondary sources that quote it.

## Claims deliberately REMOVED during fact-checking
| Removed claim (from my earlier draft) | Why |
|---|---|
| "As built, ≈30% of code" | Imprecise. NBS compares *load at collapse* to *code-expected capacity* (31%). Replaced with NBS's framing. |
| "$140 million in settlements" | Only one weak source. Removed; no dollar figure is stated anywhere in the video. |
| "In 1985…" (licensing ruling year) | Sources conflict (1984 vs 1985). Year omitted. |
| "October 1979" roof collapse date | Date not confirmed. Stated as "while the hotel was still being built". |

## Editorial and ethics decisions
- Individual engineers are **not named** on screen or in narration (not needed for the story; lowers legal risk). Roles only.
- **No depiction of victims.** People appear only as abstract dots before the collapse. The collapse is shown as line diagrams, then a silent memorial card.
- **No AI-generated "realistic" imagery** of the real event, so YouTube's altered/synthetic-content disclosure is **not required** (all visuals are diagrams and motion graphics). Disclose the AI narration voice in the description.
