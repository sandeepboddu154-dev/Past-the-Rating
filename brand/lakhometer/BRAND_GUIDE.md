# Lakhometer — Brand Guide

**Name:** Lakhometer  ·  **Line:** *Money, measured.*
**Promise:** Every rupee, shown at its real size, with every number sourced.
**Personality:** a private bank's precision with a museum curator's calm. Quiet confidence; never loud, never "get-rich".

## Identity
- **Mark:** a gold serif **L monogram** with three rising bars in its corner, wrapped by a **meter arc** (with gauge ticks) that sweeps under the L and rises into a green growth arrow breaking out of a fine gold ring. Background: deep navy with an emerald glow.
- **Wordmark:** "LAKHOMETER" in Instrument Serif capitals, gold gradient, wide tracking.
- **Lockup:** mark + wordmark + "MEASURE ◆ PLAN ◆ GROW" in tracked Inter caps. (Spoken/video sign-off stays "Money, measured.")

## Palette (use sparingly: onyx dominates, gold is jewellery)
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy Onyx | `#090D16` (lifted `#16233A`) | Backgrounds |
| Graphite | `#16171B` | Panels, surfaces |
| Ivory | `#F2ECDD` | Primary text, headlines |
| Stone | `#8E8A7E` | Secondary text |
| Growth Green | `#3FCB86` | Only the gauge growth zone and positive figures |
| Champagne Gold | `#CBA65C` (gradient `#F3E1A6` → `#CBA65C` → `#8A6626`) | Mark, key numbers, rules. **Never more than one gold focal point per frame.** |

## Typography (all SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use)
- **Headlines:** Instrument Serif (regular and italic)
- **Labels / UI:** Inter 600, uppercase, tracking 0.15–0.5em
- **Numbers:** JetBrains Mono (tabular, precise)

## Visual language (videos)
- A gallery-dark 3D set: spotlit objects, soft gold rim light, low-reflective floor.
- Objects always at true physical scale. Shoot scale comparisons with a long lens so proportions stay truthful.
- Gold marks the key number or the subject; everything else is ivory or stone.
- Engraving and guilloché textures for transitions and chapter cards.
