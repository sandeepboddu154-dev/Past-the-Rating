## Script / voiceover (final)
> This man went from 632 miles an hour to zero in 1.4 seconds. On purpose.
> December 1954. Air Force doctor John Paul Stapp straps into a rocket sled called Sonic Wind.
> The question: how hard can a human body stop?
> Rockets fire. Past 600 miles an hour.
> Then water brakes stop the sled almost instantly.
> Peak force: forty-six point two g. For a moment, his body weighed about three and a half tonnes.
> Blood vessels in his eyes burst. He was temporarily blinded.
> He recovered, then spent years campaigning for what's in every car you ride: the seatbelt.
