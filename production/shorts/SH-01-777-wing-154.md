## Script / voiceover (final)
> This wing was destroyed on purpose.
> A Boeing 777. The worst force it should ever meet in service is called its limit load. Regulators demand the wing hold one and a half times that, for three seconds, without breaking.
> So in 1995, engineers pulled the wingtips upward. Roughly twenty-four feet.
> One hundred percent. One-thirty. One-fifty. Still holding.
> They kept pulling.
> At one hundred and fifty-four percent, both wings snapped, with a bang that shook the hangar.
> Four percent past the requirement. That isn't luck. It's margin.
> And you're sitting on it every time you fly.
