## Script / voiceover (final)
> In 1945, an elevator fell seventy-five floors. The woman inside survived.
> A B-25 bomber, lost in fog, had flown into the Empire State Building.
> Injured elevator operator Betty Lou Oliver was put in a car to be brought down.
> Nobody knew the crash had damaged its cables.
> They snapped. She dropped about a thousand feet.
> Two things saved her. The falling cable piled up under the car like a spring. And air trapped in the narrow shaft compressed into a cushion.
> Today, a separate brake grabs the rails if an elevator ever overspeeds.
> Free fall was engineered out.
